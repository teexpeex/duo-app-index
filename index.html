Black bars can mean different things

  • An app may run inside a compatibility window instead of using the full available app canvas.
  • An app may fill most of the display while leaving a reserved status edge. This differs from an older, smaller compatibility window.
  • Safari and system controls occupy their own space; that space is separate from the app or webpage content.
  • Media can have aspect-ratio bars inside an otherwise full-size app.

Documented Duo support does not establish that every screen is free of unused space. An app update date alone does not establish full-canvas behavior.
