Privacy

We use basic traffic measurement to understand how many people use the directory and where visits come from.

A random browser identifier is saved in your browser’s local storage for up to 30 days. We record page loads, recent activity while the page is visible, the website visited, and the referring website’s hostname when available. This estimates unique browsers and activity within the last five minutes. We do not save IP addresses, search terms, full referrer URLs, advertising identifiers, or names in analytics.

The private analytics database runs on the directory’s existing hosting. No advertising analytics service is used. Analytics does not run when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Clearing site storage removes the browser identifier.

Suggestions you submit are stored separately in a private curator inbox for review. Please leave out personal information. Suggestions are not automatically published.
